Safe to Send · Ep 08 — Confession: my AI agents are rules

My CRM "AI agent platform" doesn't call an AI model. I wrote that in the README on purpose.

Before adding a model to anything, I now ask four questions about the task:
1. Is there one correct answer I could write as a rule?
2. Does it need natural language?
3. Does it need judgement nobody wrote down?
4. Would someone have to answer for it?

I ran 12 everyday CRM ops tasks through them.
→ 5 are pure rules (unsubscribe, quiet hours, consent, duplicates, metric drops)
→ 2 are "rule decides, model explains"
→ 2 are genuine model jobs (drafting subject lines, turning a brief into a draft segment)
→ 3 need a human (approving a 240k send, replying to an upset market)

Only 2 in 12 are pure AI tasks. That's why I started with rules.

Episodes 09 to 12 add the model, one honest step at a time.
